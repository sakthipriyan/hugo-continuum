---
title: "Choosing an Accent"
date: 2026-03-10
summary: "Why a light accent needs two different text colours."
workshop_tags: ["Design", "Accessibility"]
---

Gold on white is roughly 1.6:1 — unreadable as body or link text. So a light
accent needs a darker `accentInk` for text on white, while `onAccent` goes the
other way and becomes near-black to sit on the accent itself.

> The accent you fill with is rarely the accent you can write with.
